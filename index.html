What's in it

No upsell tier. Everything below is just the app.

  • Local sources. Post-Gazette, TribLive, WESA, PublicSource, City Paper and more.
  • A finite feed. You reach the end. There is nothing after it.
  • Speed reader. One word at a time, held still, at a pace you set.
  • Read plainly. Or don't use the reader at all. Articles work the normal way.
  • Grounding. An optional breath before heavy stories. Off by default.
  • Content notes. Know what a story holds before you open it.
  • Save for later. Articles stay on your device, not on a server.
  • Continue reading. Picks up exactly where you stopped.
  • Choose your sources. Turn any outlet off. The feed is yours.
  • Daily digest. A short catch-up on what you missed.
  • Adjustable everything. Type size, spacing, motion, haptics.
  • Works offline. Anything already fetched stays readable.

Installing on Android

Abridgd is not on Google Play yet, so the Android build is a file you install directly.

  • Download the APK from the latest release on GitHub, on the phone itself.
  • Open it. Android will ask whether to allow installing apps from your browser — this is the standard prompt for anything not from the Play Store.
  • Allow it, then install. Updates mean downloading the newer APK the same way.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. Play Store release is in progress.

No account. No advertising. No analytics watching how you read. Your saved articles and your reading history live on your phone and nowhere else.
